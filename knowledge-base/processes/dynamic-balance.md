EN: Every rotor assembly passes dynamic balance calibration on a dedicated balancing machine before assembly. The process reduces vibration and extends bearing life. Workshop footage of the dynamic balance station is available in the Virtual Factory Tour (Winding Station section).
ZH: 每个转子总成在装配前均在专用动平衡机上进行动平衡校正。该工序可降低振动、延长轴承寿命。云参观页面（绕线工位部分）可查看动平衡工位实拍视频。
